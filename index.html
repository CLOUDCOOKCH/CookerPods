Inspect the standard Bluetooth serial chooser

Ear-web uses a serial service on Nothing earbuds. No equivalent AirPods service has been established. This check only inspects a port you explicitly select; it never opens or writes to it. The chooser may include unrelated devices.

No matching port does not prove that every possible vendor service is absent. We do not guess service UUIDs.

How to interpret this check

Appearing in a chooser proves selection only. A GATT connection proves access to that connection, not access to AirPods control. This page requests only the standard Battery and Device Information services.

Missing or denied standard services do not prove the device has no private services. Noise control uses a different protocol in the inspected references; this page does not claim it can reach that protocol.

Closing or refreshing the page loses the results. Disconnecting stops this page's connection; it does not revoke the browser's saved device grant. You can remove that grant in Edge's site permissions.
